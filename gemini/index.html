Race a 1:24 scale RC car inside a high-realism 3D replica of your Data Science Lab office! Follow the Red & White FIA Curbs, the Animated Glowing Floor Chevrons, and the Floating Green 3D GPS Arrow above your car through Gates 1 to 8 for 3 laps.
